WaSP

WaSP stands for the "Web Standards Project". As of March 1st, 2013, they claim to have completed their goal: making the web open and accessible for all. The organisation was formed in 1998, in response to what they described as an "ever-escalating war between two browser makers—Netscape and Microsoft". The more the two companies tried to best one another, the less compatible their browsers became.

Realising that this would make website building more and more expensive, and deny users the access they needed to content and services online, the original co-founders banded together with a group of web designers and developers to push back against the companies.
At the time, the standards they were trying to uphold were accessibility, and low cost of web production, keeping the Internet available to as many people as possible. These standards are still upheld to this day, with accessibility still holding a key part in web design.
