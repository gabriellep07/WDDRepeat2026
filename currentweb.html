W3C Standards

The W3C web standards are exactly the standards that WaSP had come together in support of. W3C stands for the World Wide Web Consortium, and it was founded in 1994 by Tim Berners-Lee, the inventor of the World Wide Web. The W3C to this day still wish to help make the web more accessible, inclusive, and secure.
